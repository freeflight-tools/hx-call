HX airspace phone directory

HX Call

Phone numbers for Swiss HX airspaces, sorted by how close you are. HX means no fixed operating hours. The zone can be activated at 30 minutes' notice, and where one publishes a number you're allowed to check its status by phone. Useful if you don't carry a VHF radio.

Widget configurator

Same URL as above. Point your phone's camera at it to open it there, then copy it into XCTrack.

Add it to XCTrack

  1. Add a Web page widget and paste the URL above.
  2. Turn on Allow web page to access XCTrack data. This is what gives it your position.
  3. Turn on Allow tapping on the web page when locked, or you can't press the call button in flight.
  4. Turn on Disable unlocking so a stray swipe doesn't rearrange it.
  5. Set refresh rate to 0. The page updates itself.

An empty widget is normal. It draws nothing while the GPS is still getting a fix, and nothing when no zone is within range, so it stays out of your way until it has something to say. The app never goes blank: it keeps the nearest zone on screen however far away it is, because there a blank page just reads as broken.

Also: the app, no XCTrack needed

Open the app → The full list with landmarks and notes, in any browser, iPhone included. No XCTrack and no setup. Add it to your home screen and it works with no data connection, which is the point: you need signal to dial, not data.

This is a phone directory, not an airspace tool. It has no boundaries and no altitudes, so use your flight computer for those. Numbers marked unverified still need checking against the current eVFR Manual. If you can't confirm a status, the airspace is active.

Your position never leaves your phone. No accounts, no analytics, nothing sent anywhere.
